Frequently Asked Questions

What is MealizerAI?

MealizerAI helps users create personalized meal plans using profile answers, calorie and macro targets, hydration guidance, diet preferences, allergies, prep time, budget, and available ingredients.

Does MealizerAI provide medical advice?

No. MealizerAI provides general nutrition planning support only. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice. If you have a medical condition, take medication, or need clinical nutrition guidance, consult a qualified health professional.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. You can also visit Apple Billing Support.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open MealizerAI, go to the Pro or planner area, and use Restore Purchase. If access does not return after a few minutes, email support with your app version and device details.

How do I delete my account or data?

Open the account deletion instructions at Delete Account, or email support for help.

Why did meal generation fail?

Meal generation requires an internet connection and active planner access for Pro-only features. If the issue persists, email support with the approximate time of the issue and the planning mode you used.
