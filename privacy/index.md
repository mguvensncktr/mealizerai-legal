## 1. Overview and scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed when you use Mealizer AI. It covers information you provide, information generated by the app (including AI-generated meal plans and recipes), and limited technical information needed to operate the service.

**Not medical advice.** Mealizer AI is a lifestyle and planning tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical dietary advice. If you have a medical condition or allergies requiring professional guidance, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## 3. Accounts and identifiers (no login)

Mealizer AI does not require sign-in (no email, Apple or Google login). Instead, the app generates a **random unique identifier** for your installation, together with a secret that only your device holds, to store your profile, plans and shopping list on our servers. A separate random identifier is used for subscriptions (see section 4.5).

Because there are no email-based accounts, your data is managed through in-app controls (edit your profile, turn notifications off, delete your data).

---

## 4. Personal information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app, generate meal plans, and manage subscriptions and notifications.

### 4.1 Information you provide (profile and preferences)

During onboarding and in your profile you may provide:

- A first name or nickname (optional); sex; age  
- Height; weight; goals (for example lose weight, build muscle); optional goal weight  
- Activity level and workouts per week  
- Diet type; allergies; foods you dislike; favourite cuisines  
- Meals per day; cooking skill; time per meal; food budget  
- Daily water intake and reminder preferences  

From these answers the app calculates your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat and water targets. These calculations use standard formulas and are **not** made by AI.

**Health-related information.** Some fields (for example weight and allergies) may be considered health-related or sensitive under certain laws. We use them only to calculate your targets, generate your plans and provide app features.

### 4.2 Content you create in the app

- Plan requests, including optional notes you write (for example "quick dinners this week")  
- Ingredients you list when you ask for a meal from what you have at home  
- Your meal plans, recipes and any changes you make to them  
- Your shopping list and which items you have ticked off  
- Your conversations with the in-app AI assistant, Nori, and any reply you report (see section 4.3)  

### 4.3 Third-party AI processing (Anthropic)

Mealizer AI uses a third-party AI service, **Claude by Anthropic, PBC**, to write meal plans and recipes, and to power the in-app AI assistant, Nori.

**When AI processing happens**  
Only when you use an AI feature: (a) creating a meal, day or week plan, including a meal from ingredients you have at home, and (b) chatting with Nori about your plans, meals, targets and cooking, for example to swap a meal, replace an ingredient you don't have at home, or regenerate a day of a plan.

**What is sent to Anthropic**  
Only what is needed for your request:

- Your goals and your calculated calorie and macro targets  
- Diet type, allergies and disliked foods (so that unsafe or unwanted ingredients are left out)  
- Favourite cuisines, meals per day, cooking skill, time per meal and budget  
- The app language (so that dishes and ingredients are familiar where that language is spoken), the current month (for seasonal produce) and recent dish names (to avoid repeats)  
- Your plan note and the ingredients you listed, if you added them  
- For the AI assistant: your messages in that conversation, its earlier replies, and the plan, meal and targets you are asking about  

**What is not sent to Anthropic**

- Your name, sex, age, height or weight (only the targets calculated from them)  
- Your installation identifier or push notification token  
- Payment details (Apple processes all payments)  
- Photos, contacts or location (we do not collect these)  

**What we receive back**  
AI-generated content: meal plans, recipes, ingredient lists and, for the assistant, its replies and revised plans or meals.

**How we store AI-related data**  
We store your profile and the resulting plans and recipes, including revisions, so you can see them later. Your conversations with Nori are stored so you can return to them, until you delete a conversation or your data. We keep up to 30 conversations per installation; older ones are deleted automatically. If you report a reply, we store the report with that message so we can review it. We also keep usage counts (for example how many messages you sent today) to apply fair-use limits.

**Please do not share sensitive health information in the chat**  
Nori is a food and meal-planning helper, not a medical service. It does not diagnose or treat conditions; for medical questions, talk to a doctor or dietitian.

**AI provider retention**  
Anthropic states that it does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models, and that it deletes API inputs and outputs within a limited period (currently 30 days), except where it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policies or comply with the law. See Anthropic's privacy policy and commercial terms for details.

**AI outputs may be inaccurate**  
AI-generated meal plans can contain mistakes. Do not rely on Mealizer AI for medical, allergy or safety-critical decisions. Always check ingredients and consult a professional when needed.

**Your permission for third-party AI**  
The last onboarding screen tells you, directly above the button that finishes onboarding, that your answers will be shared with a third-party AI service to create your plans, with a link to this Policy. By continuing you give your permission; we record when you completed onboarding. If you do not agree, do not continue. You can withdraw your permission at any time by deleting your data in the app (section 10.2); AI features cannot work without it.

### 4.4 Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a **push token** (Expo push token) to send notifications such as "your week is ready", and your reminder preferences. Meal and water reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

### 4.5 Subscriptions (in-app purchases)

Subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card details.

We use **RevenueCat** to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives a random subscription identifier (not your name or email), your purchase and renewal history, the product you bought and your store receipt, and tells our server whether your subscription is active. We store the subscription status, product, expiry date and store environment needed to unlock paid features.

### 4.6 Usage limits, diagnostics and logs

To keep the service reliable and affordable we keep:

- Counts of how many plans each installation created and the approximate AI cost per installation per month (to apply fair-use limits)  
- Notification delivery events (for example whether a reminder was opened)  
- Server logs with timestamps, error messages and your installation identifier  

We do not use these for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

## 10. Data retention and deletion

### 10.1 Retention

- **Profile, plans, recipes, shopping list and AI assistant conversations:** until you delete them or your data in the app (at most 30 conversations are kept per installation)  
- **Push token:** while notifications are on; deleted when you turn them off or delete your data  
- **Subscription records:** while needed to unlock paid features and handle subscription issues  
- **Usage counts, logs and backups:** up to 90 days, then deleted or overwritten  

### 10.2 Deletion

You can delete your data from within the app (Profile → "Delete account and data"). We then delete your profile, plans, recipes, shopping list, AI assistant conversations, push token and related records from our database. Some data may remain in backups or logs for up to 90 days. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies.
